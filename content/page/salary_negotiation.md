**目标**:

制定系统性薪资谈判策略（Develop a structured salary negotiation strategy）
**挑战**:

1. 研究目标岗位的行业薪资基准与竞争力数据
Research industry-standard salary benchmarks and competitiveness data for the target role
2. 量化分析自身核心技能与过往成就的市场价值
Quantitatively analyze the market value of your core skills and past achievements
3. 设计包含基本工资、绩效奖金和福利的阶梯式报价方案
Design a tiered proposal covering base salary, performance bonuses, and benefits packages
4. 准备应对企业方可能提出的预算限制或替代性补偿方案的策略
Prepare counter-strategies for potential employer responses like budget constraints or alternative compensation offers
